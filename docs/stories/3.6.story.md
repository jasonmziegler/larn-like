# Story 3.6: Economic Balance and Equipment Progression

## Status
Draft

## Story
**As a** player,
**I want** equipment costs and availability balanced appropriately,
**so that** teeth collection feels rewarding and equipment upgrades provide meaningful progression paths.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Equipment pricing scaled appropriately to average teeth collection rates (death drops 16-32)
2. Multiple equipment tiers available (basic → improved → advanced) with clear progression paths
3. Rare or unique equipment occasionally appears in chests or as monster drops
4. Economic balance prevents trivial equipment acquisition while avoiding excessive grinding
5. Equipment stat bonuses provide noticeable gameplay impact without breaking game balance
6. Town merchant inventory refreshes or expands based on player progression or world events

## Background / Rationale

Epic 3 introduces multiple equipment acquisition paths:
- **Starting gear:** Basic dagger (+2 ATK) and shirt (+1 DEF)
- **Merchant purchases:** Teeth-based economy (Story 3.2)
- **Chest loot:** Random discoveries (Story 3.3)
- **Monster trophies:** Promoted monster equipment (Stories 2.3, 2.9, 3.4)

This story ensures these systems work together harmoniously through:
- **Balanced pricing:** Teeth costs align with collection rates
- **Tiered progression:** Clear upgrade paths (tier 1 → tier 2 → tier 3)
- **Meaningful choices:** Players must prioritize which gear to buy first
- **Sustainable economy:** Rewards exploration without trivializing challenge

## Dev Notes

### Current Economic State

**Teeth Sources:**
- Hero death: 16-32 teeth (randomized in DeathProcessor.ts)
- Chests: 10-50 teeth (varied by chest type)
- Teeth drop on floor: collectible by future heroes

**Teeth Sinks:**
- Merchant purchases (to be balanced in this story)
- Future: Shrine blessings (Story 2.8 uses soul energy, not teeth)

**Equipment Sources:**
- Starting gear: Dagger (+2 ATK), Shirt (+1 DEF)
- Merchant purchases (Story 3.2)
- Chest loot (Story 3.3)
- Monster trophies (Stories 2.3, 2.9)

### Equipment Tier Stat Ranges (Proposed)

| Tier | Name | Attack Bonus | Defense Bonus | Acquisition |
|------|------|--------------|---------------|-------------|
| 0 | Basic | +1 to +2 | +1 to +2 | Starting gear |
| 1 | Improved | +3 to +4 | +3 to +4 | Merchant (40-80 teeth) |

### Merchant Pricing Rationale

**Design Goals:**
- Player should afford 1 tier 1 item every 2-3 deaths (~50-70 teeth collected)
- Full tier 1 loadout (10 items) requires ~15-20 deaths (~600 teeth)
- Tier 2 items are aspirational goals requiring sustained play

**Pricing Formula:**
```
Tier 1 item price = 40 + (slotValue * 10)
  - Weapon: 60 teeth
  - Body Armor: 60 teeth
  - Helmet: 50 teeth
  - Ring: 40 teeth

Tier 2 item price = 150 + (slotValue * 30)
  - Weapon: 240 teeth
  - Body Armor: 240 teeth
  - Helmet: 200 teeth
  - Ring: 150 teeth
```

### Chest Loot Rarity Distribution

**Random Chest Loot (Story 3.3):**
- 70% Tier 1 (Improved): Comparable to cheap merchant items
- 25% Tier 2 (Advanced): Saves significant teeth vs merchant
- 4% Tier 3 (Rare): Better than merchant can sell
- 1% Tier 4 (Unique): Special one-of-a-kind finds

**Expected Discovery Rate:**
- Average 2 chests per level
- Average 1 item per chest
- Player explores 10 levels per run
- Expected 20 items found per run: ~14 Tier 1, ~5 Tier 2, ~0.8 Rare, ~0.2 Unique
- Unique item discovery: ~1 every 5 runs

### Merchant Inventory Progression (Proposed)

**Initial Stock (Available Immediately):**
- 5 Tier 1 items across various slots
- Message: `"Welcome to the merchant! I have basic supplies."`

**Unlocked at Depth 5:**
- 3 Tier 2 items added to stock
- Message: `"You've proven yourself. I have some rare items for serious adventurers."`

**Unlocked after 10 World Deaths:**
- Additional Tier 2 items, possibly 1 Tier 3 item
- Message: `"The town has grown with the influx of brave heroes. New stock available!"`
